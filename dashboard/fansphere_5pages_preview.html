The story this dashboard tells

El Clásico generates the most raw fan volume — but doesn't win on combined score. The April 2021 Clásico saw 17,089 comments (highest in the dataset), yet ranks #7 of 10 on combined engagement because it had only 3 goals. Volume tells you where fans gather. Combined score tells you which matches were "events."

The biggest goal-fests rank highest. Real Sociedad 1–6 Barcelona (7 goals) and Barcelona 5–2 Real Betis (7 goals) take the top two slots — the composite weights on-pitch excitement equally with fan reaction, and 7 goals normalises to a perfect football score.

Fan volatility tells you what the average doesn't. The volatility breakdown on the Mood Curve tab makes this explicit — non-rivalry wins have the calmest fanbases, regardless of margin.

Models & scoring formulae
The two ML touch-points and the deterministic scoring on top.
SENTIMENT
VADER (NLTK)
Lexicon + grammatical rules tuned for social-media text. Output is a compound score in [−1, +1]. We label positive when compound ≥ +0.05, negative when ≤ −0.05.
Why VADER: built for short, informal text with emoji and slang. No training data required — appropriate when the corpus is small and the task is well-defined.
CLUSTERING
KMeans (k=3, scikit-learn)
Features per author (standardised): comment_frequency, matches_covered, avg_sentiment, sentiment_volatility, positive_ratio. random_state=42. Authors with ≥3 comments.
Why k=3: elbow + silhouette gave clean separation at 3, and three clusters map to interpretable personas (Tribes tab).
COMPOSITE
Engagement Score
Combined = 0.5 × football_norm + 0.5 × fan_engagement.
fan_engagement = 0.40·volume + 0.25·affect + 0.20·volatility + 0.15·reach.
football_norm = total_goals normalised across the 10-match window.
Why these weights: volume dominates intensity; affect captures whether the conversation was loud or angry; volatility rewards back-and-forth; reach rewards cross-subreddit.
Caveats worth knowing
The model is honest about its limits.
01
Single-team bias. Both subreddits (r/barca, r/realmadrid) skew Barcelona-side; 8 of 10 fixtures involve Barcelona. Conclusions generalise to those fanbases — not to neutrals or rival-team supporters.
02
VADER is rule-based, not deep. It can't read sarcasm, language switches (Spanish ↔ English ↔ Catalan), or local idioms. A transformer model would score differently — VADER was chosen for transparency and corpus size.
03
Linking confidence is fuzzy. 91,250 of 93,298 comments sit at 0.6 confidence — sub + time match but not necessarily team mention. False positives possible; directional signal still defensible.
04
10 matches is a small N. Statistical claims are descriptive of this season, not generalisable to "La Liga" or "fans" in the abstract. Extend the dataset before drawing universal conclusions.
